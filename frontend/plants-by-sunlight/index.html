Plants for your light

Match plants to the light your balcony really gets.

Pick by light

Full sun

Six or more hours of direct sun.

  • Tulsi
  • Marigold
  • Chilli
  • Tomato
  • Hibiscus
  • Rose
  • Bougainvillea
Water more often in summer.

Partial light

Three to five hours of sun.

  • Mint
  • Coriander
  • Spinach
  • Curry leaf
  • Lemongrass
  • Geranium
A good fit for east or west-facing balconies.

Low light

Shade or north-facing balconies.

  • Snake plant
  • Money plant
  • ZZ plant
  • Peace lily
  • Ferns
  • Spider plant
Water less. Soggy soil is the main risk in shade.

Low-maintenance picks

Snake plant

Survives missed waterings and low light.

Marigold

Easy from seed and blooms in sun.

Tulsi

Tough, useful and loves Indian sun.

Money plant

Grows in soil or water and tolerates shade.

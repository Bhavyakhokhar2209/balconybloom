Questions people ask

Which plants are easiest for a beginner?

Tulsi, mint, marigold, money plant and chilli are forgiving starters.

Can I grow vegetables on a north-facing balcony?

Leafy greens like spinach, coriander and methi manage with 3-4 hours of light.

How much does it cost to start a balcony garden?

Around Rs 2,000 for a basic setup. Use the budget calculator to plan yours.

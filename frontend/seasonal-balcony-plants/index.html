Seasonal planting calendar

Timing changes by city. Use this as a starting point and adjust to your weather.

What to plant when

Summer, March to June

Heat lovers do best.

  • Okra
  • Cucumber
  • Chilli
  • Basil
  • Marigold
  • Sunflower
Use shade net in peak heat and mulch the soil.

Monsoon, July to September

Choose plants that handle humidity.

  • Tulsi
  • Mint
  • Brinjal
  • Beans
  • Coleus
  • Balsam
Check drainage and keep pots out of heavy rain.

Winter, October to February

The easiest season for beginners.

  • Tomato
  • Coriander
  • Spinach
  • Methi
  • Peas
  • Lettuce
  • Petunia
Water less and move pots to catch the sun.

Tips for pot vegetables

Feed regularly

Add compost every 3-4 weeks. Pots run out of nutrients fast.

Give climbers support

Cucumber and beans need a stake or net.

Sow in small batches

Coriander and methi keep giving if sown every 2 weeks.
